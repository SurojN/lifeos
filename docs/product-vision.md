# Product vision

LifeOS is a user-owned, privacy-first personal operating system. Its first complete workflow will preserve a private medical source document, mark extracted facts as unverified, require user review, and create a verified timeline event that links back to the evidence.

This foundation intentionally stops before upload and extraction. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, provide investment advice, guarantee projections, collect camera or microphone data, or depend on institutional integrations. AI may later assist extraction only with explicit consent, confidence labels, review, and exact source attribution.

Private PUT authorization is now part of the foundation, but uploaded objects remain quarantined and unusable until future malware and content validation is implemented. Presigned downloads are intentionally forbidden.
