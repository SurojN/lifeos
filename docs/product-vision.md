# Product vision

LifeOS is a user-owned, privacy-first personal operating system. Its first complete workflow will preserve a private medical source document, mark extracted facts as unverified, require user review, and create a verified timeline event that links back to the evidence.

This foundation now includes an optional AI-assisted extraction path for JPEG and PNG medical documents. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, provide investment advice, guarantee projections, collect camera or microphone data, or depend on institutional integrations. AI extraction requires explicit consent, confidence labels, review, and exact source attribution; unsupported documents remain available for manual review.

Private PUT authorization is now part of the foundation, but uploaded objects remain quarantined and unusable until future malware and content validation is implemented. Presigned downloads are intentionally forbidden.

Documents may be added from the central vault or from the Medical, Finance, and Travel sections. They remain categorized source objects, can be downloaded only through an authenticated server route, and can be soft-deleted by the owner; deleting an original does not silently delete already confirmed derived records.

## Helpful intelligence direction

LifeOS should become a source-grounded life companion, not a collection of forms and not an unrestricted agent. Its intelligence should help a person find facts, prepare questions, compare transparent scenarios, notice missing information, and choose a next step. Important answers must cite authorized LifeOS records, distinguish provenance, admit uncertainty, and require confirmation before any external action.

The commercial promise is a useful outcome: when an important health, family, financial, travel, or identity moment arrives, the user can quickly find the right evidence and prepare. The initial paid wedge, packaging hypotheses, validation plan, and payment constraints are documented in [commercial strategy](commercial-strategy.md).
