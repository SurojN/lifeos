# LifeOS Foundation

## North star

LifeOS helps people preserve trusted life context and make better long-term decisions without surrendering control of private data.

## Core object: Life Event

A dated, categorized fact with a source. Examples: prescription, lab report, salary, SIP contribution, trip, exam, passport renewal, or family milestone.

## Initial modules

- Timeline: trusted, sourced memory
- Health: medical history organization only
- Finance: educational long-term projections
- Vault: local source documents

## Explicitly excluded from v0.1

- AI calls
- Medical diagnosis or prescriptions
- Investment recommendations, fund custody, or guaranteed returns
- Automatic camera or microphone access
- Bank, wallet, hospital, government, email, or wearable integrations
- Background monitoring
- Selling or sharing personal data

## Future AI contract

Every AI statement must carry a source, a label distinguishing extraction from inference, a confidence level, and a confirmation step when ambiguity could affect the user.
