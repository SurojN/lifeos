# 30-Day Founder Roadmap

## Week 1: founder dogfooding

Use safe personal data to create at least 20 events, 5 health records, 3 retirement scenarios, and 10 sample documents. Record every moment where data entry feels annoying or the output feels genuinely useful.

## Week 2: interviews and structure

Interview five people about lost medical history, document management, retirement uncertainty, and long-term planning. Add event-to-document links and structured fields only after repeated needs appear.

## Week 3: security design

Create the threat model, authentication plan, encryption/key-recovery design, deletion policy, consent ledger, and audit-log schema before adding cloud storage.

## Week 4: first safe intelligence

Prototype one opt-in capability: document expiry detection, source-linked extraction, or retirement-scenario explanation. Nothing extracted becomes trusted data without user confirmation.

## Validation target

Ten testers, each with at least ten events; six return within one week; five say losing the timeline would be painful; zero silent collection; zero uncited AI claims.
